Assignment 2

By Serena Beddoe

The most important thing I learned from this assignment was that the choice and preparation of input images matters as much as the code. My hybrid image of the same person at different ages came out worse because one photo had a busy background, and my happy/shocked hybrid only worked once I aligned on the glasses frames instead of the eyes, since the expressions changed the eye positions. The filtering was the same in every case, so the quality came down to picking and aligning the photos well.
