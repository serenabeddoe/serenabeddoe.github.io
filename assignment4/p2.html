Part d: Image Rectification

Looking at the rectified images, I noticed that the epipolar lines are now horizontal and the corresponding points are at approximately the same vertical positions in both images. This makes it easier to find corresponding points because instead of searching across the entire image, we only need to search horizontally along the corresponding epipolar line. As a result, the search is reduced from two dimensions to one dimension, making it easier to match points between the two images.
